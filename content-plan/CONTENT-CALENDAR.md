3. 3-5 internal links (to homepage, /casino-2/, /tariffs/, related posts)
4. At least 3 images with keyword-rich alt text

6. CTA button: "Book Your Casino Experience" linking to /tariffs/
7. Table of contents for posts >2000 words
8. Author byline: "Casino Pride Team"
9. Category: "Blog" or "Casino"
10. Minimum 3 relevant tags (from existing tag library)

### Internal Linking Map:
- Every post links to: Homepage (anchor: "best casino in Goa")
- Every gaming post links to: /casino-games/ or /goa-casino-games/
